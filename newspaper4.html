Kevin Priestley, Actor

A British-Australian performer of stage and screen practice

 •    
Mr. Priestley, of London

From north-east Tasmania to the working rooms of Melbourne, and latterly to London, Kevin Priestley has followed a steady course toward performance. Raised among artists and itinerant makers, he took early to stories, then to the discipline of the body and the voice.

He served briefly in the Australian Army and later practised as a Registered Nurse. These seasons, though apart from the stage, left their mark: a practical bearing, and a habit of attention.

His present work spans feature films, short pictures, television, and commercial employment, with several engagements announced for the coming seasons.
