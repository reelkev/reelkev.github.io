Kevin Priestley

BIOGRAPHY

  • Ethnicity: British Australian
  • Languages: English
  • Accents: Australian, USA
  • Skills: Bodybuilding, Boxing, Nurse, Australian Army

Kevin Priestley, a British-Australian actor was born and raised in north-east Tasmania, Australia, and now resides in Melbourne. He grew up in a forested area where his father hosted gatherings for artists and creatives. As a child, he attended festivals, assisting with cooking and distributing chapati, an Indian flatbread, and was influenced by the Rainbow Family.

He farmed sheep with his grandfather, learning land-management and bushcraft skills. Kevin served briefly in the Australian Army, gaining discipline and teamwork experience. Homeschooled during childhood, he later attended college and university as an adult. He developed an early interest in photography and videography. In addition to acting in film, television, and short films, he works as a Registered Nurse and has skills in bodybuilding and boxing.
