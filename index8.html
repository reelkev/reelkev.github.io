Biography

Ethnicity: British Australian
Languages: English
Accents: Australian, American
Skills: Bodybuilding, Boxing, Nursing, Australian Army

Kevin Priestley, a British-Australian actor, was born and raised in north-east Tasmania and now resides in Melbourne. Growing up in a creative environment influenced by artists and the Rainbow Family, he developed a deep appreciation for storytelling. He served briefly in the Australian Army and worked as a Registered Nurse while pursuing his acting career.
